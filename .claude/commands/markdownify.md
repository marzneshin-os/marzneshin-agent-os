**MARKDOWNIFY DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE**

Convert any document type into LLM-optimized Markdown:
1. **Convert File**: `python3 scripts/markdownify_cli.py convert <path>`
2. **Save Output**: `python3 scripts/markdownify_cli.py convert <path> -o <target.md>`
3. **Inspect Size & Tokens**: `python3 scripts/markdownify_cli.py inspect <path>`
