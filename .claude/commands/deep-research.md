**DEEP RESEARCH PROTOCOL**

Conduct systematic, multi-source research:
1. **Scoping**: Define precise research hypotheses and boundary questions.
2. **Tavily Search**: Use `tavily_search` (Khairallah Tool #16) for clean, high-signal technical sources.
3. **Context7 Query**: Use `context7` (Khairallah Tool #17) for official library documentation.
4. **Triangulation**: Validate claims across at least 2 independent sources.
5. **Evidence Ledger**: Record all source URLs, timestamps, and quotes.
6. **Synthesis**: Structure findings with clear Markdown tables, diffs, and trade-offs.
